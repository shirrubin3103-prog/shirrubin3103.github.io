How it works

01 · INTAKE FORM
Customer submits quote form
Captures home size, cleaning type, and contact details in one clean form (e.g., Jotform).
02 · TRACKER DATABASE
Automation logs the record
Creates a structured row in your tracker (e.g., Airtable or Google Sheets) with zero copy-pasting.
03 · OWNER NOTIFICATION
Owner gets an instant alert
Sends the summary straight to your preferred channel (e.g., Telegram, WhatsApp, or SMS).
Automation organizes the inquiry and notifies the owner; the business owner remains responsible for pricing and the customer response. Any platforms of your choice can be used for each step.
